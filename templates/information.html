❤️ About Heart Health

Understanding heart health is an important step toward maintaining a healthy lifestyle and becoming aware of cardiovascular risk factors.

What is Heart Disease?

Heart disease refers to conditions that affect the heart and its normal functioning. Some cardiovascular conditions can develop gradually and may be influenced by different health and lifestyle factors.

❤️ Common Risk Factors

Several factors may be associated with cardiovascular risk, including age, high blood pressure, cholesterol levels, blood sugar, physical inactivity, smoking, obesity and family history.

🩺 Important Health Indicators

Health measurements such as blood pressure, cholesterol, resting heart rate and exercise response can provide useful information when assessing cardiovascular health.

💗 Healthy Heart Habits

Regular physical activity, balanced nutrition, adequate sleep, avoiding tobacco and maintaining healthy blood pressure and cholesterol levels can support overall heart health.
