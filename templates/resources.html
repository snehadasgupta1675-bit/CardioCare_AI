📚 Heart Health Resources

Explore useful information and simple practices that can help you learn more about cardiovascular health.

❤️ Know Your Risk Factors

Learn about common cardiovascular risk factors such as age, blood pressure, cholesterol, blood sugar, physical inactivity and smoking.

🥗 Healthy Eating

A balanced diet containing vegetables, fruits, whole grains and appropriate sources of protein can support overall heart health.

🏃 Stay Physically Active

Regular physical activity can support cardiovascular fitness and contribute to a healthier lifestyle. Choose activities that are appropriate for your fitness level.

😴 Healthy Lifestyle

Getting adequate sleep, managing stress and avoiding tobacco can contribute to better overall cardiovascular health.

🩺 Regular Health Checkups

Regular health checkups can help monitor important measurements such as blood pressure, cholesterol and blood sugar.

⚠️ When to Seek Medical Help

If you experience concerning symptoms such as chest pain, severe breathing difficulty or fainting, seek appropriate medical attention promptly.
